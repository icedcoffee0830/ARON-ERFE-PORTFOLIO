# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React / Next.js (user's choice). Hosting is undecided, so the site stays portable and must not depend on features tied to one host.

## Users

Peers, other designers, and the general public who come to look at the designer's work. They browse and judge the craft. The work is the reason they are there.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for a designer/creative. It exists to show the work well and to make the designer's craft memorable. It succeeds when a visitor spends time with the projects and leaves with a clear sense of who made them and how they think.

## Positioning

A multidisciplinary designer who works across brand/identity, UI/product, and graphic/print. The site itself is evidence of their design judgment. Open: the one-line personal statement and specialty emphasis are not yet written by the user.

## Operating Context

Visitors mostly arrive from shared links and social or design communities, and browse on desktop and mobile. Each project is presented on its own (case study or gallery), and the work spans three disciplines, so the structure must present very different formats together: identity systems, interface screens, and print/poster pieces.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Project collection that can be filtered or grouped by discipline: brand/identity, UI/product, graphic/print.
- An individual page per project for images and a written description.
- Content is still being collected. The structure must make adding projects easy (data-driven, not hard-coded per page).
- Undecided: the display name / brand name, domain, host, contact method, and whether there is an about page, CV, or writing section.

## Brand Commitments

None confirmed yet. The display name is still to be confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

Some finished project work is ready. The rest is still being collected. No image paths have been provided yet. Future work must use clearly marked placeholders for missing projects and must never invent clients, testimonials, awards, press, metrics, or collaborators.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads: the interface serves the projects and never competes with them.
2. Present each discipline in its native form, so posters read like posters and interfaces like interfaces.
3. The site is itself a portfolio piece; its craft is part of the argument.
4. Easy to grow: adding a project should be a content change, not a redesign.
5. Only what is real: no fabricated proof.
